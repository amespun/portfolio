Hi, I’m Aïda.

I’ve spent 9+ years designing B2B and B2C products for startups and scale-ups in Spain, Germany, the UK, the US and the Netherlands. I like the messy part: aligning product, engineering and sales on what to build, then proving it with research and numbers.

I started in graphic design, and 14+ years in design later I still care about the last pixel.
