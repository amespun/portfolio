Luxury Service Concierge

Branding Concierge Luxury is a Concierge Agency that curates and crafts high-end brand experiences for luxury businesses around the world. From fashion to hospitality, they elevate their clients’ presence through intentional design and storytelling.

Work done
Logo creationBranding & Art DirectionNo-code Website

My role was to define a refined, high-impact visual identity that speaks to the elegance and exclusivity of their offering and the creation of the no-code website. I led the creative direction, balancing timeless sophistication with a modern edge. The final brand system includes a flexible logo suite, custom typography, and a rich, editorial-inspired palette. Every element was built to convey confidence, craftsmanship, and impeccable taste, hallmarks of the luxury industry.
