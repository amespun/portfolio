Company

Tekman Education

Tekman Education is a Barcelona-based company that creates innovative educational programs for students from early childhood to secondary school, using methods like problem-based learning and gamification. Their programs support over 600,000 students across Spain and Latin America, with digital tools for teachers, students, and families.

At Tekman, I worked first as a Graphic Editorial Designer across several projects, and later transitioned into a Product Designer role, contributing to their digital platforms for kids and learning experiences.
